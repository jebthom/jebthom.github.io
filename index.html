— About

I am an HCI/AI MSc student under Professor Fanny Chevalier at the University of Toronto. I also completed my BSc in Computer Science at the University of Toronto with a focus on artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on understanding and enhancing human-AI creative collaborations through novel interface and interaction designs, with particular emphasis on protecting users' agency, ownership, and trust in AI-assisted creative work.

— Teaching

Teaching & TA work.

As a graduate student, I have been involved in teaching and TA work across several courses in computer science and HCI at the University of Toronto. My focus is on helping students build practical skills in research methods, interactive systems design, and working with AI tools thoughtfully.

More details on specific courses and TA positions coming soon. If you're a UofT student interested in HCI research, feel free to reach out.
